Protocols

Fire Emergency

Follow these steps immediately if you spot a fire or hear an alarm.

  1. Stay calm. Do not panic.
  2. Activate the nearest fire alarm if not already ringing.
  3. Evacuate the building using the nearest safe exit.
  4. Avoid elevators. Use the stairs.
  5. If there is smoke, stay low to the ground.
  6. Proceed to the designated assembly point.

DO ✅

  • Alert people around you
  • Check doors for heat before opening
  • Follow responder instructions

DON'T ❌

  • Go back inside for belongings
  • Use the elevator
  • Hide in restrooms or closets
