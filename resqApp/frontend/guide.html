Protocols

Fire Emergency

Follow these steps immediately if you spot a fire or hear an alarm.

  1. Stay calm. Do not panic.
  2. Activate the nearest fire alarm.
  3. Evacuate the building using the nearest safe exit.
  4. Avoid elevators. Use the stairs.
  5. Proceed to the designated assembly point.

DO ✅

  • Alert people around you
  • Check doors for heat

DON'T ❌

  • Go back inside
  • Use the elevator

Traffic Accident

Steps to take if you are involved in or witness a vehicle collision.

  1. Ensure your own safety first. Move to the side of the road.
  2. Turn on hazard lights to warn other drivers.
  3. Check for injuries and call emergency services.
  4. Do not move severely injured persons unless in immediate danger.

DO ✅

  • Take photos of scene
  • Exchange info

DON'T ❌

  • Flee the scene
  • Stand in traffic

Medical Crisis

What to do if someone collapses, is injured, or has a sudden illness.

  1. Check the scene for danger before approaching.
  2. Check if the person is responsive.
  3. Call for an ambulance immediately.
  4. Keep the person calm and still until professionals arrive.

Flood / Weather

Protocols for severe weather or flooding.

  1. Move immediately to higher ground.
  2. Avoid walking or driving through floodwaters.
  3. Unplug electrical appliances.
