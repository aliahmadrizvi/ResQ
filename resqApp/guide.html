Protocols

Fire Emergency

Follow these steps immediately if you spot a fire or hear an alarm.

  1. Stay calm. Do not panic.
  2. Activate the nearest fire alarm.
  3. Evacuate the building using the nearest safe exit.
  4. Avoid elevators. Use the stairs.
  5. If there is smoke, stay low to the ground.
  6. Proceed to the designated assembly point.

DO ✅

  • Alert people around you
  • Check doors for heat before opening

DON'T ❌

  • Go back inside for belongings
  • Use the elevator

Traffic Accident

Steps to take if you are involved in or witness a vehicle collision.

  1. Ensure your own safety first. Move to the side of the road if possible.
  2. Turn on hazard lights to warn other drivers.
  3. Check for injuries on yourself and others.
  4. Call emergency services immediately if there are injuries.
  5. Do not move severely injured persons unless they are in immediate danger (e.g., car fire).

DO ✅

  • Take photos of the scene for records
  • Exchange information if involved

DON'T ❌

  • Flee the scene
  • Stand in the middle of active traffic

Medical Crisis

What to do if someone collapses, is injured, or has a sudden illness.

  1. Check the scene for danger before approaching the victim.
  2. Check if the person is responsive by tapping their shoulder.
  3. Call for an ambulance or medical help immediately.
  4. If trained, begin CPR or apply first aid (like pressure to bleeding).
  5. Keep the person calm and still until professionals arrive.

DO ✅

  • Provide clear location details to dispatch
  • Keep the patient warm and comfortable

DON'T ❌

  • Give them food or water
  • Move them if you suspect a neck/spinal injury

Flood / Weather

Protocols for severe weather, heavy flooding, or natural disasters.

  1. Move immediately to higher ground or higher floors.
  2. Avoid walking or driving through floodwaters (6 inches of moving water can knock you down).
  3. Unplug electrical appliances to prevent electrocution.
  4. Listen to local alerts and official campus/city guidance.
  5. Stay away from power lines and electrical wires.

DO ✅

  • Prepare an emergency kit
  • Follow designated evacuation routes

DON'T ❌

  • Drive into flooded roads
  • Touch electrical equipment if you are wet
